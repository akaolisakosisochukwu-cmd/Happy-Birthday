You are the constant thought that anchors my world, especially when the distance feels like it is building a bridge too long to cross. There are so many things I cherish about you, but on this day, the anniversary of the first breath you took, my heart overflows with gratitude that you simply exist.

Your strength makes me feel safe. Your mind challenges mine. Your tenderness is a sanctuary I never knew I needed. Every laugh we share is a song that stays with me, and every glance we give through a screen is a promise I know we will keep.

I wish I could gift you the entire world today. I would wrap up every ocean that separates us, and give you the shortest path back to me. Until then, my love, I hope this little place, a reflection of everything you mean to me, keeps you warm until my arms finally can. All I want is for you to feel celebrated, seen, and utterly adored, today and forever. You are my home, my peace, and my greatest adventure. I love you, so, so much.
